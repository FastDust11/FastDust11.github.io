The application is used solely to create, manage and restore encrypted backups from the owner's systems to the owner's Google Drive account. It is not intended for third-party access.

The owner is responsible for selecting data for backup, safeguarding repository passwords and credentials, monitoring backup completion, maintaining recovery information and periodically verifying that files can be restored.
